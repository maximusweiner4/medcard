## Full Description
KinRx helps you manage medications for yourself or someone you care for. Add medications from a searchable database of thousands of drugs, track refills, check for drug interactions, and share a read-only medication list with doctors and family.

FEATURES:
★ Add and organize medications with dose, form, route, frequency, prescriber, and pharmacy details
★ Refill reminders when you're running low — see upcoming refills at a glance
★ Drug interaction checker powered by NIH RxNav — check all active medications at once
★ Share a secure QR code or link with any healthcare provider — no login required for them
★ Export a full PDF medication list for doctor appointments
★ Manage multiple patients — track medications for family members, parents, or patients
★ Caregiver access — invite family members to view or co-manage a patient's medications
★ Full change history — see every edit with who made it and when
★ Biometric lock — fingerprint or face unlock to protect sensitive health data
★ Works offline — data cached locally when you lose signal

KinRx is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult your healthcare provider before making changes to your medications.
